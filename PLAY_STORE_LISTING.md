**Notes that live on your phone.**

Iron Rabbit is a fast, friendly, offline-first productivity app for people and families who want their notes, chores, meal plans, shopping lists, and pantry inventory in one place — without a subscription, without an account, and without their data leaving the device.

Everything you write stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. There are no ads, no trackers, no analytics. You can work in airplane mode all day and everything still just works.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Notes, done right**

• Rich notes with checklists, colours, categories, reminders and pinned tiles
• Drag-and-drop reordering, filter by category, group by mood
• Instant search across everything
• Photo and voice attachments stored on-device as offline blobs
• Templates for anything you repeat weekly
• Distraction-free full-screen editor when you want to focus

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Kid Mode**

• Age-appropriate chore dashboard with streaks, stars and rewards
• Kids see chores in a big, tappable, celebratory UI
• Parents approve completed chores to unlock rewards
• Optional PIN protection per child
• Confetti when a chore is approved — real motivation, no dark patterns

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Shopping & Pantry**

• Real supermarket-department shopping lists that don't lose your place
• Barcode scanner (offline-capable via Google ML Kit)
• Product info via Open Food Facts: Nutri-Score, NOVA processing level, ingredients, allergens, and additive warnings — so you can spot palm oil, artificial colours or worrying E-numbers in one tap
• Full pantry inventory with fridge / freezer / cupboard zones, expiry tracking, restock lists

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Meal Planner**

• Drag recipes into a weekly grid across breakfast, lunch and dinner
• 15 built-in recipes to start; add your own with ingredients
• "Generate shopping list" turns the whole week into a departmentised list in one tap

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Backup, Restore, and Peace of Mind**

• One-tap export to a single offline .json — take it anywhere
• Merge or Replace on import so you can transfer between phones or restore after a factory reset
• Nothing leaves your device unless you decide to share the file

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Built for privacy, on purpose**

• No account required. Ever.
• No ads. No analytics SDKs. No trackers.
• PINs are salted and hashed on-device — never stored in plain text
• Secure biometric unlock (fingerprint or face)
• Optional Privacy Screen — hides app content in the Android task switcher
• Panic PIN — a decoy PIN that opens a locked, empty state if you're ever forced to unlock

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Works fully offline**

Iron Rabbit stores everything in IndexedDB on your device. Reminders fire from the OS. Barcode scans go through Google ML Kit locally. The only optional network call is to Open Food Facts when you scan a food product — and even that is opt-in per scan.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Who it's for**

• Parents juggling chores, dinners and grocery runs
• Students who need offline note-taking without a login wall
• Anyone who's tired of subscription-based productivity apps
• People who care about digital privacy without wanting to configure anything

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Free. No trials. No paywall.**

Iron Rabbit is free to download and free to use. Everything above ships in v1.0.

Try it, keep your notes on your phone where they belong, and let us know what to build next.

## Data Safety Form — Answers

Google Play requires this before submission. Iron Rabbit's answers are simple because we don't collect anything.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** (HTTPS for the single OpenFoodFacts optional call) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **Yes** — Settings → Data & Reset, or uninstall the app |

If Play asks about individual categories:

- **Personal info** → not collected
- **Financial info** → not collected
- **Health & fitness** → not collected
- **Messages** → not collected
- **Photos & videos** → collected **only on-device**; user chooses per-attachment; not shared, not uploaded
- **Audio files** → collected **only on-device** (voice notes); not shared, not uploaded
- **Files & docs** → collected **only on-device**; not shared, not uploaded
- **Calendar / Contacts** → not collected
- **App activity** → not collected
- **Web browsing** → not collected
- **App info & performance** → not collected
- **Device or other IDs** → not collected

Play's own definition of "collect" = *transmitted off the device*. Since Iron Rabbit does not transmit user data off the device (Open Food Facts sees only the barcode you scan, not any personal identifier), **the correct answer to every "do you collect X" question is No.**
